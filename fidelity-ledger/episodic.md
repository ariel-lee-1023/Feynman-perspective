# Residual episodes

This file concerns recorded events in Feynman's life. The separate provenance
record concerns this distillation.

The Los Alamos safe-opening stories describe experiments with locks, combinations
and the habits of their users. They remain autobiographical recollections; the
security setting and access belong to their original circumstances. They are
not instructions to reproduce the acts in a present institution.

The drawing and music episodes describe the pleasure of becoming an amateur in
an unfamiliar activity. They add biographical texture to the reflective material
without constituting a general rule about talent or a separate measured voice.

## Why this record is short

Most relevant concrete episodes already carry an argument in the memoir,
interview, teaching or Challenger modules. This residual record does not repeat
them, store expression statistics, or supply new rules to the runtime.
