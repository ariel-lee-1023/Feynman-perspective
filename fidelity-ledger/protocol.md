# Round-two evaluation protocol

Frozen at the time in freeze.json, before this iteration’s new extraction; this is not a retroactive correction of round one. Old final groups retire to development, with original files/hash records preserved. Original train remains eligible. Three selected sole-author papers use seed 42 and whole-work groups, reserving related detailed expositions in each research program from construction. Two correlated projection probes per work produce only one development and one final source group; this is narrow technical-reasoning coverage. General mentions of helium, Bose symmetry, and hadrons exist in the old training books; the held-out article arguments and dated proof note were not found there. A new work is not an entirely unfamiliar scientific topic. Pretrained familiarity is uncontrolled and requires the minimal-role baseline.

The public inventory contains metadata only; construction must not read private/. Parent elected not to read the optional polaron train paper. The 1953/1969 source pages were OCRed, and tested pages visually checked; prior-article fragments on both first pages are excluded from the Feynman-only derivatives. OCR equations are not an authoritative formula source. Source projection and scope tasks use separate item contexts so a scope fact pack cannot leak into another projection prompt.

New behavioral tasks are transfer scenarios grounded in old training commitments, not new independent attestation of those commitments. The cost rubric preserves disclosure/publication conditions and real stated incentives. Scope tests use source-dated fact packs; they measure period/condition/attribution handling with supplied facts, not unprompted historical recall. Identity has three tasks per phase, target plus Carl Sagan and Karl Popper; minimal target baseline is reported separately. The asymmetry in evidence between full target package and minimal neighbors is explicit. Two distinct reviewers receive anonymized shuffled answers, common prompts/facts and candidate names; neither condition nor truth key. Keep both identity choices, even disagreements. Other disputed interpretations require human review, not a fabricated consensus.

Every task/condition must start a new model message list/context with only the task, condition role, and candidate-permitted runtime. No other task answers or rubric/target text. Candidate and baseline use identical actual model/settings/task facts/tool permissions; record what is actually observable, using unknown fields where unavailable. A fresh manual subagent is not automatically equivalent to the runner’s filesystem/tool isolation or provider-level reproducibility. Do not label manual records executable-runner proof. The frozen task/rubric hashes are in freeze.json.

Run development first; curation may use development findings. Freeze runtime hash, reconfirm development on final bytes, and only then claim final groups immediately before their first dispatch. Save both condition answers before blind grading. Any final-result-informed revision retires that final to development and needs new untouched final groups. Never overwrite old result hashes or fabricate missing model/settings. Final gate floors: projection .50 and no baseline regression; reasoning .70 (2/2 for two cases); commitment .80 (2/2); all three scope cases pass all criteria; identity macro .70 with no candidate below .50. Register/style are separate evidence and can remain unresolved; passing technical projection cannot repair them.

The release-evidence wording alone does not specify per-item granularity, but evaluation-runner.md explicitly requires it. Per-condition old observations remain honestly qualified observations rather than being erased. Before-extraction means before the new construction being evaluated; no method can undo prior legacy extraction. Style-only external spoken-source work, if acquired, is a separate supplemental sealed comparison and will preserve its later freeze timestamp.
