# Scope decision after the three-unit finding

The three-unit teaching comparison remains insufficient. Its only unstable pair is the introductory lecture collection versus the remedial review lectures. The law/introductory pair agrees across all three disjoint windows.

The final statistical claim is therefore explicitly narrower: a default voice for introductory scientific explanation, supported by the two general-audience/introductory lecture sources. Remedial review, memoir, public exhortation and technical reports remain sources of reasoning and dated decisions, not members of that calibrated family. This limits what is claimed; it does not delete the contrary analysis or assert a single voice for allFeynman writing.

This restriction was chosen after diagnostic measurement, before style generation and final testing. It is a development-informed scope change. Default thresholds remain unchanged. The ensuing two-unit run is confirmatory of the scoped recorded pair, not independent validation of the selection procedure. Held-out style evaluation must assess that narrowed claim separately.
